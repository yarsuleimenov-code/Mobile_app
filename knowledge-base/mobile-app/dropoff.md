# How to Complete a Dropoff

**Use this article when:** you deliver cargo and need to confirm its condition and handoff.

1. Open the Dropoff stop or search the **Order number** on the Dropoff screen.
2. Compare the displayed place count, weight, volume and **Pickup photos** with the physical cargo.
3. Add at least one **Delivery photo**.
4. Select **Cargo matches pickup photos** only when it does. Choose **No visible damage**, or **Report damage instead** and enter details.
5. Select **Confirm Dropoff**. Open **Delivery review**; if Pickup is not locked, complete Pickup review/signing first.
6. Review Delivery evidence and exceptions. Choose **Sign on device** or **SMS code** and provide the driver name. Each party enters an optional **Your comment** on their own signing screen. For SMS code, enter **Contact comment (reported)** before verification; the driver comments before their signature. In the prototype `111111` is always invalid.
7. Complete the contact confirmation, obtain the Zaberman driver signature, select **Complete Order eBOL** and open **View POD**.

**Expected result:** the local Dropoff is confirmed, both document snapshots are locked, and POD is available.

**Important:** “Dropoff confirmed” and “Order eBOL completed” are separate steps. An unsigned Supplemental Pickup blocks Delivery confirmation. Documented damage does not itself block the handoff.

**If something goes wrong:** “Order not found” means this browser state has no Pickup record for the number. Recheck the Order; do not select a different one to bypass the message. See [User Guide: Dropoff](../../docs/mobile-app/user-guide.md#dropoff).
